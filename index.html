English

The developer of "Holy Night Survivors" (the "App"), DevQuark, respects your privacy.

  1. What the developer collects: The developer does not collect or store any personal information, such as your name, email, location or contacts, on its own servers.
  2. Game save data: Your progress (gold, upgrades, achievements) is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer. It is deleted when you uninstall the App.
  3. Ads (Android version): The Android version offers optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch. They are served by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and your interactions with ads in order to show and measure ads. This is handled under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Where consent is required (for example the EEA and the UK), the App shows a consent form on first launch. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. The PC (Steam) version has no ads.
  4. In-app purchase (Android version): The "Ad-free Pack" is sold through Google Play billing. Google processes the payment; the developer never receives your payment details. The App only stores on your device whether you own the pack.
  5. Sharing: Apart from the ad serving above, the developer shares no user information with third parties.
  6. Children: The App is not directed at children.
  7. Changes: Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
